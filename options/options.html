Privacy by design

Protection is designed to run locally in your browser.

  • Request filtering is handled by your browser.
  • No account, analytics endpoint, or BuildMintZ server is required.
  • Filtering rules are bundled with this extension release.
  • BuildMintZ Shield is not a full antivirus and cannot guarantee detection of every malicious website, tracker, or advertisement.
